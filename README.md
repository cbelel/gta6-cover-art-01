# Vice City Cover Maker

Turn your photos into an original after-hours magazine cover. Arrange six photos in a scattered-print collage, try Filmstrip 5 or Classic 3, adjust each crop, choose four color grades, and write your headline. Illustrated city postcards, stickers, halftone, grain, and skyline details are drawn directly on canvas.

Run `node serve.ts --port 8080` and open `http://localhost:8080`. No build step or network requests are needed. Photos stay in your browser. Export square, portrait, or landscape PNGs at full resolution. Drag a photo to pan or use its sliders. Anton is bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License.

Run `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/vice-city-output node main.ts` to package the app and its four screenshots with a deterministic dossier.
